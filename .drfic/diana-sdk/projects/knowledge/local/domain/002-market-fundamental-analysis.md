## TL;DR

Fundamental analysis evaluates the underlying business quality and financial health of an instrument or company. In Diana, it should inform long-term conviction, risk assessment, and opportunity ranking without becoming a stand-alone verdict.

## Conceptos Clave

- Revenue: the top-line income generated by a company.
- Earnings: profit after expenses.
- Valuation: how expensive or cheap a business appears relative to its fundamentals.
- Growth: how fast the business is expanding.
- Quality: the stability and resilience of the business model.

## Core Concepts

### Business Quality
Fundamental analysis focuses on whether the company behind the instrument is strong, stable, and capable of sustaining value creation. That includes profitability, balance sheet strength, moat, and management quality where applicable.

### Valuation and Relative Comparison
No valuation ratio should be treated in isolation. Ratios such as P/E or PEG are useful only when compared against sector context, growth expectations, and business quality.

### Long-Term Signal Context
Fundamentals are especially useful for ranking opportunities and filtering out weak candidates. In a Diana workflow, they provide the broader conviction layer beneath shorter-term signal cores.

## Integration with the Project

For diana-inversions, fundamental analysis is one of the selectable cores used to support confluence-based buy/sell logic. It should contribute to opportunity screening, confidence weighting, and risk explanation for the user.

## Decisions de Diseño

- Use fundamentals as a ranking and conviction layer.
- Avoid reducing complex businesses to one metric.
- Keep the analysis explainable for user review.
- Separate company quality from short-term price movement.
